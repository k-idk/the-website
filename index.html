About Our Team

About Us

"We are students celebrating Diwali through entrepreneurship, creativity, and authentic handmade craftsmanship. At Bright Sparks, our torans and cotton files are lovingly handmade by one of our own mother, so every purchase supports a local artisan and a family’s Diwali. This is our first time running a stall, and we’re learning the value of collaboration and enterprise as we go. Come say hi and take home a little festive joy!"
